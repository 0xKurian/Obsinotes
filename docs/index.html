How to Use NotePages in Obsidian 🚀

Simple, fast, and completely integrated into your daily vault workflow.

01

Create a Notebook

Click the ribbon notebook icon or press Ctrl+P to start a new digital notebook in any vault folder.

02

Annotate or Ink

Choose Cornell, ruled, or import a PDF background. Write notes, snap geometry, and type LaTeX formulas freely.

03

Export & Share

Export any page or the entire notebook to vector PDF, 300 DPI PNG, or SVG. Your data stays 100% in your local vault.
