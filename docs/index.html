Core Features & Capabilities

A dedicated digital notebook workflow engineered specifically for note-takers, researchers, and students.

✍️

Hardware-Accelerated Inking

Ballpoint, Fountain, and Pressure-sensitive pens powered by perfect-freehand. Real canvas multiply highlighters layer softly without obscuring text.

📑

Native PDF Annotation

Import multi-page PDF documents directly into your vault. Highlight text, draw diagrams in the margins, and export back to vector PDF with all handwritten annotations preserved.

📐

Hold-to-Snap Geometry

Draw circles, rectangles, triangles, or connector lines with your stylus. Pause for 350ms to automatically snap your freehand stroke into clean, vector-sharp geometry.

📌

Pastel Sticky Notes & Wikilinks

Drag-and-drop color-coded sticky notes with multi-line text. Type native Obsidian [[Wikilinks]] to connect handwritten boards directly to your markdown vault.

∑

LaTeX Math Formulas

Type inline formulas like $E=mc^2$ or full display blocks directly onto your notebook pages with automatic MathJax rendering.

🖨️

300 DPI Print & Vector Export

Export individual pages or entire multi-page notebooks to print-ready vector PDF, high-resolution PNG (up to 300 DPI), or scalable vector SVG anytime.

PDF Study & Annotation Workflow

Turn any document in your vault into an annotated digital notebook in seconds.

STEP 01

Import Any PDF

Right-click any PDF in your Obsidian file explorer and choose Import PDF as notebook.

STEP 02

Highlight & Write

Use the highlighter to mark key concepts and pen tools for margin notes with full stylus pressure sensitivity.

STEP 03

Export with Annotations

Export the annotated document back to PDF with your handwritten notes and highlights permanently embedded.

Installation & Quick Start

Method 1 (Recommended): In Obsidian, open Settings → Community Plugins → Browse, search for NotePages, click Install and Enable.

Method 2 (Manual / GitHub Release): Download main.js and manifest.json from GitHub Releases, place them in .obsidian/plugins/notepages/ and reload Obsidian.

To create your first notebook: Click the notebook ribbon icon on the left, or press Ctrl/Cmd + P and run NotePages: New notebook.
