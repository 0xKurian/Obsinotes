Simple Workflow

Get Started in Less Than 60 Seconds

Seamlessly integrated into your Obsidian Vault without complex setup.

01

Create a Notebook

Click the notebook icon in your ribbon or press Ctrl+P to open a new digital notebook in any vault folder.

02

Choose Paper or PDF

Pick Cornell, ruled, grid, or import any PDF document. Ink with natural pressure, snap shapes, and type LaTeX formulas freely.

03

Export & Share

Export individual pages or entire notebooks to print-ready PDF, 300 DPI PNG, or SVG. Your notes stay 100% local in your vault.
