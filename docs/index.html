Features

Engineered for Serious Thinking

A complete digital notebook workflow engineered specifically for note-takers, researchers, and students.

✍️

120 FPS Inking Engine

Ballpoint, Fountain, and Pressure Pen with micro-tap dot sensitivity. Real-feel multiply highlighters layer softly without hiding words.

📑

PDF Margins & Bookmarks

Import slide decks or multi-page textbooks as high-resolution background pages. Take margin notes and highlight key definitions.

Hold-to-Snap Geometry

Draw circles, boxes, or arrows and pause for 350ms. NotePages automatically straightens your freehand stroke into crisp vector shapes.

Pin color-coded sticky notes anywhere on your page. Connect directly to other markdown notes with native Obsidian Wikilinks.

Type inline formulas or display math blocks directly onto handwritten pages. Ideal for engineering, physics, and statistics notes.

300 DPI Print Export

Export single pages or entire multi-page notebooks to vector PDF, ultra high-resolution PNG (up to 300 DPI), or scalable SVG.

Get Started in 3 Steps

Installed in seconds through Obsidian Community Plugins.

1

Install from Obsidian Community Plugins

Open Obsidian → Settings → Community Plugins → Search for NotePages → Click Install and Enable.

2

Open a New Digital Notebook

Click the notebook ribbon icon on the left, or press Ctrl/Cmd + P and type NotePages: New notebook.

3

Choose Cornell, Ruled or Import PDF

Pick your favorite paper template from the toolbar or import a textbook PDF to begin handwriting immediately with Apple Pencil or stylus.
