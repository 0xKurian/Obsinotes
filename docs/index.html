Engineered for Digital Notebook Craft

Every tool is tailored to feel natural under a stylus while staying deeply tied to Obsidian's linked notes.

High-Precision Inking

Ballpoint, Fountain, and Pressure Pen powered by perfect-freehand. Sensitive micro-dot detection and layered multiplier highlighters.

Hold-to-Snap Shapes

Draw circles, rectangles, triangles, or arrows freehand and pause for 350ms. NotePages instantly snaps them into crisp geometric vectors with customizable fill colors.

Sticky Notes & LaTeX

Color-coded pastel sticky notes with soft shadows. Type inline math $E=mc^2$ and Obsidian Wikilinks [[Note]] natively.

PDF Annotations

Import multi-page PDF documents directly as notebook backgrounds. Highlight passages, write notes in the margins, and re-export without vector resolution loss.

Multi-Format Exporter

Export pages to 300 DPI print-ready PDF, high-res PNG, or pure scalable SVG. Save directly into your vault with zero name collisions.

Memory Virtualization

Smart culling releases off-screen canvas contexts in notebooks with 100+ pages. Scroll continuously without frame drops, memory spikes, or GPU exhaustion.
